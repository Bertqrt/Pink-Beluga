Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag on the Shop page, then press the order button in the bag. Your order is sent to us and we reply to confirm the total, delivery and payment.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order we share payment options with you, such as mobile money or bank transfer. We never ask for card details on this website.

How long does delivery take?

Ready-made items usually ship within 2–4 days. Custom pieces take 1–2 weeks depending on the design. We will give you a date when we confirm.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes, please! Pick “Custom request” in the form above and describe your idea, colours and budget.
