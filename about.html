Our Story

A little pink whale with big dreams.

How it started

Pink Beluga began with a paintbrush, a love of all things blush, and friends who kept asking, “Where did you get that?” What started as gifts for people close to us grew into a small studio making art, jewellery and accessories that feel personal.

The beluga is our mascot because belugas are gentle, social and always look like they are smiling. That is exactly how we want you to feel when you open one of our packages.
